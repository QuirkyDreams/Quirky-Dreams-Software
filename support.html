Support

Help for installing, opening your Messages archive, searching, redacting, and exporting conversations.

System requirements

Xtractor is a macOS application. The Mac App Store listing will contain the current minimum supported macOS version.

Opening your Messages archive

  1. Launch Xtractor.
  2. Choose your Apple Messages folder when prompted.
  3. Grant Contacts access only if you want Xtractor to show familiar contact names instead of raw phone numbers or email addresses.

Xtractor uses sandboxed, user-granted access and does not modify your Messages database.

Try the Sample Archive

If you want to explore Xtractor without opening your own Messages data, choose Open Sample Archive from the welcome screen. The sample archive contains synthetic conversations and can be used to test search, redaction, and export features.

Attachments are missing

Xtractor only previews attachments that are available locally on your Mac. If Messages references an attachment that is only in iCloud and is not stored locally, Xtractor will show that it is unavailable rather than downloading it automatically.

Search does not find a recent message

Allow Xtractor a moment to update its local search index after opening or refreshing the Messages archive. You can also rerun the search from the main search field.

Exports

Xtractor supports PDF, Word, and CSV exports. For very large conversations, export may take time because the app processes messages in batches to avoid loading the entire conversation into memory.

Redactions

Text and image redactions are stored separately by Xtractor and never alter the original Messages database or attachment files. Exported PDF and Word documents can include the applied redactions.

Reset Xtractor's local data

If you need to clear Xtractor's saved search index, redactions, saved searches, and remembered Messages-folder permission, use Delete Xtractor Local Data and Quit inside the app.

Still need help?

Visit the Contact page and include your macOS version, Xtractor version, what you were trying to do, and any error message you saw.
