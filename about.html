ABOUT QUIRKY DREAMS!

Creative minds. Technical solutions.

Sometimes the software you need already exists. Sometimes it almost exists. And sometimes your problem is peculiar enough that the right answer is to build something around the way you actually work.

We like those.

Quirky Dreams! Studios brings 20+ years of professional video and audio production experience together with emerging creative technology and custom software development. We start with the problem rather than the technology: what is getting in your way, what would make the workflow better, and whether custom software is actually a sensible solution.

Questions before cameras, code, or AI.

A good project starts with understanding the job it needs to do. Before building, we ask questions, define what success looks like, check feasibility, and agree on scope. If the idea makes sense, we build in stages so working versions can be tested and refined along the way.

Video, creative technology, and custom software.

We edit and produce video, create motion graphics and audio, explore AI-assisted visual workflows, and build focused software for specialized needs. The right solution depends on the story, audience, and practical problem.

Some of our work

Text Curator grew from a need to search, review, preserve, and export Apple Messages conversations on a Mac. Xtractor adapts that kind of workflow around a client's particular requirements. Other projects explore different kinds of specialized tools and ideas.
