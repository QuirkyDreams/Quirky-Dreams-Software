Xtractor

A private, local-first tool for searching, reading, redacting, and exporting Apple Messages conversations.

Search

Search across conversations, names, numbers, dates, and attachments, with in-conversation highlighting and navigation.

Preserve

Export conversations to PDF, Word, or CSV, including large archives through streaming export.

Redact

Save text and image redactions without modifying the original Messages database or attachments.

Designed for local processing

Xtractor works with Messages data on your Mac after you choose the Messages folder. The app does not require an account and does not send your Messages content to Quirky Dreams! Software.

Important: Xtractor never modifies your original Apple Messages database. Exports and Xtractor-specific local settings are stored separately.
