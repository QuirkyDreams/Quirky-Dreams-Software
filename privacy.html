Xtractor

Privacy Policy

Xtractor is designed around local processing. Your Messages content stays on your Mac unless you choose to export it somewhere yourself.

Effective date: August 30, 2026

Overview

Quirky Dreams! Software does not collect, transmit, sell, or share the content of your Apple Messages conversations through Xtractor. Xtractor does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, telemetry, or an account system.

Messages access

Xtractor asks you to choose the Apple Messages folder on your Mac. This access is used to read the Messages database and locally available attachment files so Xtractor can display, search, redact, and export your conversations.

Xtractor opens the Messages database read-only and does not modify the original Messages database or source attachments.

Contacts

If you allow Contacts access, Xtractor uses Contacts locally to replace phone numbers and email addresses with familiar names. Contact data is not uploaded to Quirky Dreams! Software.

If you do not grant Contacts access, Xtractor can continue to work using phone numbers or email addresses instead.

Local search index and app data

To improve performance, Xtractor may create local app data on your Mac, including:

  • a local search index for Messages content;
  • saved text and image redactions;
  • saved search definitions;
  • appearance preferences;
  • a security-scoped bookmark used to remember the Messages folder you selected; and
  • a synthetic sample archive used for demonstration and App Review.

This data is stored locally on your Mac and is not transmitted to Quirky Dreams! Software.

Exports

When you export a conversation to PDF, Word, or CSV, you choose where the exported file is saved. Exported files remain under your control. Xtractor does not upload exported files to Quirky Dreams! Software.

Attachments

Xtractor only uses attachment files that are available locally on your Mac. It does not automatically download missing iCloud attachments. Image redactions are applied to Xtractor's rendered/exported copies and do not alter the original attachment.

Apple Maps links

If a Messages conversation contains an Apple Maps location share, Xtractor may display a cleaner, human-readable location label. When you click it, macOS opens the original Apple Maps link. Xtractor does not geocode the address or send it to Quirky Dreams! Software.

Data collection

Xtractor does not collect personal data for Quirky Dreams! Software. In particular, Xtractor does not transmit your Messages, Contacts, search terms, redactions, attachments, exported files, or usage history to us.

Retention and deletion

Xtractor's local app data remains on your Mac until you delete it, uninstall related local files, or use Xtractor's Delete Xtractor Local Data and Quit command.

That command removes Xtractor's local search index, saved redactions, saved searches, stored Messages-folder permission, and sample archive. It does not delete Apple Messages, Contacts, or exported files you created.

Third parties

Xtractor does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The app may ask macOS to open user-selected files or links using normal system services.

Children

Xtractor is a general-purpose productivity utility and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If Xtractor's privacy practices materially change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.
