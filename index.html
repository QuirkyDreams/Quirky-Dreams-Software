BEFORE THE DETAILS

Want an NDA first?

A nondisclosure agreement (NDA) is a written agreement about how private project information may be used and shared. If you’d like one, request it before sending sensitive details. We’ll prepare an agreement that identifies the parties and the purpose of the conversation.

WORKING TOGETHER

Clear terms from the start.

01 / OWNERSHIP

Who owns the finished work?

We define deliverable ownership and usage rights in the project agreement. Your supplied materials remain yours; pre-existing tools and third-party elements retain their applicable rights and licenses.

02 / PAYMENT

How do payments work?

We agree on scope, deliverables, schedule, and pricing before production. Projects may be fixed-price or hourly depending on the work. Changes outside the agreed scope are discussed before additional work begins.

The signed project agreement governs the exact rights, payment, delivery, revisions, and support terms.
