BEFORE THE DETAILS

Want an NDA first?

A nondisclosure agreement (NDA) is a written agreement about how private project information may be used and shared. If you’d like one, request it before sending sensitive details. We’ll prepare an agreement that identifies the parties and the purpose of the conversation.

WORKING TOGETHER

Clear terms from the start.

01 / OWNERSHIP

Who owns the app?

Your idea, content, and data stay yours. For custom work, we agree in writing who owns the finished app and source code; client ownership after full payment is our usual approach. Quirky Dreams! keeps its pre-existing tools and reusable components. Third-party software follows its own licenses.

02 / PAYMENT

How do payments work?

We scope and price work in stages, starting with discovery and moving to a prototype and build when it makes sense. Before each stage, you’ll know its deliverables, price, and payment schedule. Changes outside the agreed scope get a separate quote.

The signed project agreement sets the exact ownership, payment, delivery, and support terms for your project.
