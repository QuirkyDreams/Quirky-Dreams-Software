Text Curator

A private, local-first tool for searching, organizing, preserving, and exporting Apple Messages conversations.

Search

Search across conversations, participants, dates, message text, and attachments, with in-conversation highlighting and navigation.

Preserve

Export conversations to PDF, Word, or CSV. Export presets and batch export help turn large archives into useful, readable records.

Work privately

Text Curator processes your Messages data locally on your Mac. It does not require an account and does not send your Messages content to Quirky Dreams! Software.

Built for real Messages archives

Text Curator is designed to work with both everyday conversations and very large histories. Search filters, saved searches, attachment navigation, conversation-style exports, and export summaries help you find and preserve the material that matters.

Attachments stay honest

If Messages references an attachment that is not stored locally on your Mac, Text Curator reports it as unavailable rather than substituting another file. For historical attachments that may still be available through Messages, Text Curator can help you locate the surrounding conversation so you can download the original in Apple Messages.

Designed for local processing

Text Curator works with Messages data on your Mac after you explicitly choose the Messages folder. It opens the Messages database read-only and never modifies your original Apple Messages database or source attachments. Exports and Text Curator-specific settings are stored separately.
