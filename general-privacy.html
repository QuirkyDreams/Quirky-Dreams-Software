General privacy.

This page covers our website and general inquiries. Each app has its own privacy information.

Information you send us

This website has no account or contact form. If you email us, we receive your address and the information you choose to share. We use it to respond and discuss your inquiry or project. Please ask for an NDA before sending sensitive project details if you prefer one.

Website hosting

Our site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Our pages do not include our own analytics or advertising trackers. For GitHub’s practices, see its privacy statement.

App privacy and questions

Our apps have separate privacy information. Find your app’s policy and support links. For questions about this website or an email you sent us, contact support@quirkydreams.com.
